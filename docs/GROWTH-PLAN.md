# BENAI: first paying customers without an audience

## Offer

Sell a useful assistant for a narrow task: cafe/service-business FAQs, a small shop’s policies, a course guide or a creator’s project introduction. Lead with fewer repeated questions and a clear enquiry handoff. Do not lead with photorealistic realtime promises the CPU cannot meet.

The free local studio demonstrates the product without buying provider subscriptions. Customers who already pay for Gemini, OpenAI, ElevenLabs or HeyGen can bring their own keys. Their bills stay with them. Existing Creator/Business plans sell hosted workflow, integrations, limits and support; verify pricing against real hosting/support costs before enabling checkout. Manual admin plan grants allow a small trial before payment activation.

## In-product funnel

1. Create a template assistant with accurate business facts, hours, policies and contact details.
2. Try it in the studio. Confirm it answers the actual customer questions and admits missing facts.
3. Publish an opt-in demo. Share its link where the owner has permission to share it.
4. Visitors try chat and optionally consent to send an enquiry to the owner.
5. The owner sees session/turn counts and private leads. A BENAI signup link attributes referrals.
6. Upgrade only when the integration/support value is clear; no referral cash rewards or fabricated traffic are promised.

The dashboard counts hashed demo sessions and chat turns over 30 days; leads and referred signups are account totals. It does not identify anonymous visitors or store their chat transcripts. Lead consent is recorded; delete leads when no longer needed. Owners remain responsible for lawful, relevant follow-up.

## First two weeks

Use five invited businesses or creators. Find prospects through communities where you participate and public directories; research them manually, build a useful sample, and ask permission before sharing/promoting it. No follower list is required. Do not scrape personal addresses, automate spam or claim customers that do not exist.

Publish a short honest demo showing fast CPU export, live conversation and a lead handoff. Use the four templates as examples. Offer a bounded assisted setup session, then ask what question or task it saved. Share customer quotes only with permission.

Review activation (assistant created + demo published), sessions, enquiries, attributed signups, repeated usage, support time and hosting cost. These implemented counts help evaluate the funnel; they do not guarantee sales. If nobody returns, improve the use case before paying for ads or a GPU.

## Staffing and spend

Founder: customer research, setup, permissions, support and release decisions. Use the built-in operations and backup workflows. When revenue supports it, commission a limited independent security/accessibility review of the deployed origin. No hiring, paid advertising, cloud purchase or external outreach was performed by this release.
